---
title: Callouts
description:
  Highlight notes, warnings, and tips in MDX with Mantine-styled callouts
---

Callouts draw attention to important information. They map to Mantine `Paper`
with a colored accent bar and work in any MDX page.

## Types

<Callout type="info" title="Info">
  Use for neutral context or background information.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Warning">
  Use when something might break or needs caution.
</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="Error">
  Use for mistakes, deprecations, or hard failures.
</Callout>

<Callout type="success" title="Success">
  Use when confirming a step worked or a recommendation is safe.
</Callout>

<Callout type="idea" title="Idea">
  Use for tips, shortcuts, or optional improvements.
</Callout>

## In your MDX

```mdx
<Callout type="info" title="Heads up">
  Your content here.
</Callout>
```

The `warn` type is normalized to `warning` internally, so both spellings behave
the same.

## Styling

Callouts are styled with CSS Modules on top of Mantine surfaces, so they stay
readable in light and dark mode without Tailwind.
