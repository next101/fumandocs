Fumandocs adds a small set of MDX components on top of Fumadocs, styled with
Mantine. They are available in every document out of the box.

## Callout

<Callout type="info" title="Tip">
  Use callouts to highlight notes, warnings, and ideas. The `info`, `idea`, and
  `warn` variants all follow your Mantine theme.
</Callout>
